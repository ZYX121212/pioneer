Interactive globe geography: Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 country polygons (public domain), from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. City markers use approximate city-centre coordinates for resource discovery; they do not represent administrative boundaries.

China globe coverage uses DataV.GeoAtlas areas_v3 province-level geometry: https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/100000_full.json (retrieved 2026-10-06). All 34 provincial-level areas, including Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao, plus the source's maritime feature are preserved. These replace Natural Earth's separate China/Taiwan geometries as one China navigation group. Source polygon vertices are retained; ring winding is normalized for the spherical renderer.
